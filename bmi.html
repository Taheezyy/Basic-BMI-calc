About this calculator
  • BMI cannot tell muscle from fat, so a muscular person can have a high BMI without excess body fat.
  • Bone density is not included, because it cannot be estimated from height, weight, age and sex.
  • Ages 16 to 19 use BMI-for-age percentiles; ages 20 and over use adult BMI categories and an averaged body-fat estimate.
